🎁 From My Heart 💖 - A Digital Birthday Surprise
Welcome to "From My Heart", a sweet and personal web-based birthday gift that you can customize and surprise your girlfriend, bestie, or sister on their special day!

🌟 About the Project
This is more than just a webpage—it's a virtual hug, a bundle of heartfelt emotions, and a memory box designed with love. With glowing wishes, floating hearts, a warm message, a surprise box, and nostalgic photo slideshows—this project brings a big smile to someone you love.

🎯 Who It's For
Whether it's your:

👩 Girlfriend – to show her how much she means to you

👭 Best friend – to celebrate the bond you share

👧 Sister – to make her feel like the queen she is

This digital surprise makes their day unforgettable 💐

💫 Features
🧁 Birthday wish popup with candle animation

💌 Secret heartfelt message with beautiful fonts

🎁 Surprise gift box reveal with floating hearts

📸 Slideshow of your favorite moments together

🎵 Background music for a magical vibe

💗 Responsive and works on mobile

✨ Make It Personal
Want to add your own magic? You can:

Change the name and birthday wish

Add your own heartfelt letter in message.html

Replace slideshow images with your own collection

Use it again and again for different loved ones!

💡 Sometimes the best gifts don’t come in a box—they come from the heart. This one does both.
